Screen-free gifts for tween girls: 10 ideas

  1. A guided crush journal. Prompts make journaling feel fun instead of like homework.
  2. A craft kit she can finish in a weekend. Quick wins keep it fun.
  3. A big puzzle. Good for a cozy weekend.
  4. A friendship bracelet or bead kit. Social and creative.
  5. Card and board games. Especially ones for 3+ players.
  6. A sketchbook and good pencils. For the doodler.
  7. A book series. Book one plus a note to ask for book two.
  8. Outdoor gear. A new bike helmet, a jump rope, a scooter.
  9. A baking or cooking kit. Something you make together.
  10. A disposable or instant camera. Photos without the phone.
💡 Tip: pair the journal with a set of gel pens. It doesn't come with pens.

Questions

What are good screen-free gifts for tweens?

Journals with prompts, craft kits, puzzles, card games, books and outdoor gear. The most-used ones feel personal or social.

Is a journal a good screen-free gift for a tween?

Yes, especially a guided one. Rate My Crush gives her prompts and a scorecard, so it's fun to fill out instead of a blank page.
