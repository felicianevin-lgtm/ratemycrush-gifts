Valentine's Day Gifts for Girls That Aren't Candy (Ages 7–14)

Candy is gone by February 15th. If you want a Valentine's gift for the girl in your life that lasts a little longer, here are ideas that still feel festive.

Rate My Crush: a crush journal for girls 7–14

It's practically made for Valentine's Day, and unlike candy hearts, she'll still have it next year. It's a guided journal: every crush gets a page with a code name, a 10-category star scorecard (Smile, Eyes, Hair, Style, Personality, Humor, Kindness, How They Treat Me, Smarts/Grades and Bonus Spark, 50 stars total), a Crush Level heart meter, and prompts like "Best Thing About Them." 100 pages, 6×9.

Valentine's gifts for girls (not candy): 10 ideas

  1. A crush journal. About as on-theme as Valentine's gifts get.
  2. Heart-shaped or pink stationery. Cards to hand out to friends.
  3. A cozy pair of pajamas. For a Valentine's movie night at home.
  4. Friendship bracelet supplies. Galentine's gifts for her friends.
  5. A Valentine's craft kit. Make cards, garlands or decorations together.
  6. A pink or red hair accessory set. For a festive school day.
  7. A book about friendship. Something sweet to read.
  8. A fun mug with a note. Fill it with something small.
  9. A baking kit. Heart cookies, together.
  10. A handwritten letter from you. The one she'll keep.
Tip: pair the journal with a set of gel pens. It doesn't come with pens.

Questions

What's a good Valentine's gift for a daughter that isn't candy?

Something she'll keep: a journal, pajamas, a craft kit or a handwritten letter. Rate My Crush is a crush journal for girls 7–14, so it's about as on-theme as Valentine's gifts get.

Is Rate My Crush appropriate for Valentine's Day?

Yes. It's a lighthearted, age-appropriate journal for girls 7–14 to rate their crushes with stars and a heart meter.
