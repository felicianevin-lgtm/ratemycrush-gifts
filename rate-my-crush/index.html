📝 How it works

One crush. One page. Zero chill.

1
🕵️

Give them a code name

Every crush gets a page: code name, where they're seen most, their squad, what they're into and their faves.

2
⭐

Rate them in 10 categories

Smile, Eyes, Hair, Style, Personality, Humor, Kindness, How They Treat Me, Smarts/Grades and Bonus Spark. Up to 5 stars each.

3
💜

Find the Crush Level

Add up the stars (50 max) for Tiny Crush, Pretty Cute, Big Crush or Totally Obsessed. Then the prompts: best thing about them, funniest memory, why we'd be a good match.

Questions grown-ups ask

What age is Rate My Crush for?

It's made for girls ages 7 to 14.

What's inside the journal?

100 pages. Each crush gets a page with a code name, where she sees them, their squad and their favorites, a 10-category star scorecard (Smile, Eyes, Hair, Style, Personality, Humor, Kindness, How They Treat Me, Smarts/Grades and Bonus Spark, for 50 stars total), a Crush Level heart meter, and prompts like "Best Thing About Them" and "Why I Think We'd Be a Good Match."

What's the difference between Playful, Moody and Floral?

Only the style. All three have the same prompts inside, each styled to match its cover. Playful is bright pastel with a glossy cover, Moody is dark and edgy with a matte cover, and Floral is navy florals with a matte cover.

How big is it?

It's a 6×9 inch paperback. The Playful Edition is also available in hardcover.

Where can I buy it?

Rate My Crush is sold on Amazon. Search "Rate My Crush" or use the links on this page.

Does it come with pens, stickers or a lock?

No. It's the journal itself. A set of gel pens makes a nice pairing if you're giving it as a gift.

Is it a good gift?

It makes a fun gift for birthdays, Christmas stockings, Valentine's Day and sleepovers: a fun, screen-free way for her to process first crushes, and a keepsake she'll laugh about when she's grown.
