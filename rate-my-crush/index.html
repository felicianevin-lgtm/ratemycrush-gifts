Rate My Crush: the crush journal for girls 7–14

A guided journal where she rates her crushes on a 10-category scorecard, tracks her Crush Level, and answers prompts she'll laugh about when she's grown. By Felicia Nevin. Sold on Amazon.

Quick facts

  • Ages: 7–14
  • Pages: 100
  • Size: 6×9 inch paperback (Playful also in hardcover)
  • Editions: Playful (bright pastel, glossy), Moody (dark and edgy, matte), Floral (navy florals, matte). Same prompts inside, each styled to match its cover.
  • Each crush page: code name, where she sees them, their squad, their favorites, a 10-category star scorecard, a Crush Level heart meter, and prompts.
  • Scorecard categories: Smile, Eyes, Hair, Style, Personality, Humor, Kindness, How They Treat Me, Smarts/Grades, Bonus Spark (50 stars total).
  • Crush Level meter: 1–15 Tiny Crush · 16–30 Pretty Cute · 31–40 Big Crush · 41–50 Totally Obsessed.
  • Prompts include: "Best Thing About Them" and "Why I Think We'd Be a Good Match."
  • Where to buy: Amazon (search "Rate My Crush").

Inside the journal

Why people give it

It's a fun, screen-free way for girls to process first crushes, and the pages she fills at 11 turn into a keepsake she'll read out loud at 30. It makes a fun gift for birthdays, Christmas stockings, Valentine's Day and sleepovers.

Questions

What age is Rate My Crush for?

It's made for girls ages 7 to 14.

What's inside the journal?

100 pages. Each crush gets a page with a code name, where she sees them, their squad and their favorites, a 10-category star scorecard (Smile, Eyes, Hair, Style, Personality, Humor, Kindness, How They Treat Me, Smarts/Grades and Bonus Spark, for 50 stars total), a Crush Level heart meter, and prompts like "Best Thing About Them" and "Why I Think We'd Be a Good Match."

What's the difference between Playful, Moody and Floral?

Only the style. All three have the same prompts inside, each styled to match its cover. Playful is bright pastel with a glossy cover, Moody is dark and edgy with a matte cover, and Floral is navy florals with a matte cover.

How big is it?

It's a 6×9 inch paperback. The Playful Edition is also available in hardcover.

Where can I buy it?

Rate My Crush is sold on Amazon. Search "Rate My Crush" or use the links on this page.

Does it come with pens, stickers or a lock?

No. It's the journal itself. A set of gel pens makes a nice pairing if you're giving it as a gift.

Is it a good gift?

It makes a fun gift for birthdays, Christmas stockings, Valentine's Day and sleepovers: a fun, screen-free way for her to process first crushes, and a keepsake she'll laugh about when she's grown.
