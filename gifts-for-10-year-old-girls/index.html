Gift Ideas for a 10-Year-Old Girl (That She'll Actually Use)

Ten is a funny age to shop for. She's too old for a lot of toys, not quite into teen stuff, and she has opinions. The best gifts at this age let her feel a little more grown-up while still being fun. Here are ideas that tend to land.

Our pick

Rate My Crush: a crush journal for girls 7–14

Ten is right around when crushes start becoming a Whole Thing, and this gives her a fun place to put them. It's a guided journal: every crush gets a page with a code name, a 10-category star scorecard (Smile, Eyes, Hair, Style, Personality, Humor, Kindness, How They Treat Me, Smarts/Grades and Bonus Spark, 50 stars total), a Crush Level heart meter, and prompts like "Best Thing About Them." 100 pages, 6×9.

Gifts for a 10 year old girl: 10 ideas

  1. A crush journal. A guided journal where she rates her crushes on a scorecard. Funny now, priceless later.
  2. A big set of gel pens or fine-tip markers. Every journal kid needs the good pens.
  3. A friendship bracelet kit. Something to make, trade and wear.
  4. A cozy blanket or oversized hoodie. Comfort gifts never miss at this age.
  5. A room upgrade. String lights, a small mirror, a fun pillow: anything that makes her room feel like hers.
  6. A beginner craft or sewing kit. Hands-on projects that take an afternoon.
  7. Card games for sleepovers. Easy group games she can bring to a friend's house.
  8. A chapter-book series. The first book of a series she can keep reading.
  9. A mini instant camera or photo album. For the friend-group photo wall.
  10. An experience. Tickets, a class, or a one-on-one day out with you.
Tip: pair the journal with a set of gel pens. It doesn't come with pens.

Questions

What do 10-year-old girls like?

At 10, many girls are into friendships, crafts, journaling, room decor, sleepovers and anything that feels a little more grown-up. Gifts that let her express herself tend to be the biggest hits.

What's a good screen-free gift for a 10-year-old girl?

Journals, craft kits, books, card games and room decor are all screen-free favorites. A guided crush journal like Rate My Crush is a fun one because she'll want to fill it out.
